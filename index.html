Next-Word Predictor Sandbox

Understand how Large Language Models (LLMs) generate text by predicting next words/tokens based on training data.

Note:Modern LLMs use deep neural networks (transformers) with billions of parameters. This app uses a simplified (n-gram) frequency model to illustrate the core concept of next-token prediction.

Step 1

Training Data

Provide training data (example sentences), one per line. The model learns the probabilities of the words that follow other words.

Step 2

Learned Probability Table

This table displays the model's learned vocabulary and the exact probability distribution for words following each word.

Model not trained yet. Provide training data and click "Train Model".

Step 3

Model Inference & Generation

Provide a prompt and run inference to observe how the model generates words step-by-step based on word probabilities.
